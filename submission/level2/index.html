Method

Each cow is compared with its pen-mates on 13 behavior indicators linked to BRD in the literature (feeding and bunk attendance, lying, activity, rumination, drinking, social isolation, herd synchrony, grooming). Deviations in the sick direction are weighted by strength of evidence and shrunk when the cow was seen for little time. A reasoning agent then checks the top cows on video for visible signs (head and ear carriage, nasal discharge, breathing) and for data artifacts. A self-check accepts only signs backed by a close enough image. "top-3 stability" is how often the cow stays in the top 3 when the observed time is resampled. Full method: docs/LEVEL2_METHOD.md.
