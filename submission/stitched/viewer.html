End-to-end checks

Computed from the stitched files when this page was built. "Check" items are not failures, but need a look before the result is trusted.

    Every cow, every minute

    One row per cow, one column per minute. The dark line is where one chunk hands over to the next. Hover a cell for the minute's label, evidence and why; click a legend entry to show only that behavior.

    The joins up close

    20 minutes either side of a chunk join. If the ids line up, most cows keep doing what they were doing across the line. The mark after each cow says how its id was joined: ✓ by overlapping boxes, ! boxes with the cameras disagreeing, ? appearance only.

    Time budgets

    Share of each cow's visible time per behavior over the stitched window (minutes the cow was not visible are left out; the right column says how many hours that is).
